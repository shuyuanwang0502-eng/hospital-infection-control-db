Conceptual model (EER)

Made from the written requirements. Tests and evidence samples are weak entities of an infection case, and every person is either a doctor or a patient (total, disjoint specialization).

__EER_SVG__
Double box: weak entityDouble diamond: identifying relationshipDouble line: total participationDashed: derived attributeD: disjoint specialization

Relational schema

Doctors and patients use the id of people as their key. Tests and samples have a composite foreign key to case_patient, so they can only point to patients who are in the same case.
